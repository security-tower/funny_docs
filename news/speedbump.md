News Report
===========
    
    
Dept. of Transportation to place speed bumps on interstate highways.
--------------------------------------------------------------------

<p class="georgiatext">We'll see what happens. We have modeled (and used wind tunnels on) cars,
and observed that going fast enough to die from going over a speed bump
results in extreme lift forces, lifting the sedan off of the road before it hits the speed bump. We,
with the help of the FAA,  enforced altitude and air speed limits for
roads. - Marty "MartDonald" McDonalds Hoy, CEO of the Dept. Of 
Transportation.</p>
