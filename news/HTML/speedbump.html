Dept. of Transportation to place speed bumps on interstate highways. --------------------------------------------------------------------

We'll see what happens. We have modeled (and used wind tunnels) cars, and observed that going fast enough to die from going over a speed bump results in extreme lift forces, lifting the sedan off of the road. We, with the help of the FAA, enforced altitude and air speed limits for roads. - Marty "MartDonald" McDonalds Hoy, CEO of the Dept. Of Transportation.
